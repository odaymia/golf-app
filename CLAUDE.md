# Scribble Golf Club

Scribble Golf Club (repo `golf-app`) is a golf app for leagues, daily tournaments, and foursomes playing betting games
on the course. Goal: everything Golf Genius does, but far easier to use. Ships
with a website (marketing page + public league/event pages).

Separate product from the shop apps (time clock, Shop Desk). Own repo, own
deploy, own Supabase project.

## Plan

1. Foursome MVP: pick course, add players, pick games, hole-by-hole scoring,
   live settle-up ("Mike owes Dave $14"). **Built (Phase 1).**
2. Leagues: season schedule, pairings, standings, points, league handicaps.
   **Built:** league builder, invite codes, rounds, matches, standings.
3. Tournaments: flights, shotgun/tee times, big leaderboard, side pots, payouts.
   **Built:** tournaments (one-day or multi-round; stroke, Stableford, teams,
   Ryder Cup) with live leaderboards. Not yet: flights, tee times, payouts.
4. Business: club/organizer accounts, branding, paid tiers, App Store builds.
   **Started:** Capacitor iPhone/Android projects, store prep in docs/app-store.md.

## Stack

Two pages from one Vite build: the website at `/` (`index.html`, static HTML
+ `src/site/`) and the app at `/app/` (`app/index.html`). The app is Vite 5 +
React 18 (plain JS, no router) as an offline-first PWA
(`vite-plugin-pwa`, service worker precaches the app). Rounds and courses live
on the device in IndexedDB. Supabase project `qdnrmeipmggpxynggsnl` holds
accounts, profiles, saved players and leagues (casual-round sync and realtime
leaderboards come later). Capacitor later for App Store / Play Store.

### Engine conventions

- All money is integer cents. Every game returns `balances` `{playerId: cents}`
  that must sum to exactly zero; `computeRound` throws if one doesn't.
- Game configs live on the round (`round.games[]`), including in-round state
  (Nassau `presses`, Wolf `decisions`), so a round is one JSON document.
- Games score "as if the round ended now": results update hole by hole.
  Nassau and match status count holes in order and stop at the first gap.
- Plus handicaps are stored as negative indexes (+2.1 -> -2.1).
- Rule choices (document any change here and in the module header):
  - Nassau auto-press: a new press starts the hole after the latest bet in that
    nine goes N down, only if a hole is left in the nine. The 18-hole bet is not
    auto-pressed unless `pressOverall`. Presses pay the segment stake.
  - Team bets (2v2): each winner collects the stake, each loser pays it.
  - Skins per-skin: each other player pays the winner. Validation = winner must
    tie or win the next hole; skins on 18 need none; ties after 18 go unwon.
  - Wolf: lone/blind wolf wins or loses stake x multiplier with each of the 3.
  - Vegas: a score of 10+ goes first; birdie flips the other team unless both
    teams birdie; net scores below 1 count as 1.
  - Split match (casual, 2 vs 2 teams only): `game.segments`
    `[{ from, to, format }]` (best ball 'fourball', combined 'shamble',
    'singles'); each stretch is its own bet worth `stake`, a singles stretch
    is two bets (first vs first, second vs second). Paid as of now like every
    match. Setup: "Split the round" switch under Match play (2 vs 2 teams);
    no scramble/alternate shot in casual rounds (scores are per player).

## Accounts

- Sign-in is a 6-digit email code (`signInWithOtp` + `verifyOtp`), not a
  magic link (password sign-in also exists, mainly for the store reviewers'
  demo account): a link would open Safari instead of the home-screen app. The
  Supabase email templates (Magic Link and Confirm signup) must include
  `{{ .Token }}`. The built-in mailer allows only a few emails per hour; add
  custom SMTP before a league signs up.
- The session is stored on the phone, so the app opens signed in with no
  signal. First sign-in requires a name (first-time Profile screen).
- `profiles` (one per user) and `saved_players` (private to the owner) are
  protected by row level security in `supabase/schema.sql`. Schema changes go
  in that file; the user runs it in the SQL Editor.
- Never ship the service_role/secret key. GHIN: handicap index and GHIN number
  are self-reported until the USGA approves an integration; that integration
  must run server-side (e.g. a Supabase Edge Function), never in the app.

## Leagues

- `leagues.settings` (jsonb) holds the format; `normalizeSettings` fills
  defaults and drops invalid combos. Units: individual / teams (2 or 4) /
  ryder (two named sides). Scoring: stroke / stableford / match, net or
  gross. Season: points (by finish, ties split, optional best-N weeks;
  FedEx Cup style: each round is `tier` regular or major, majors pay
  `majorMultiplier`x; `fieldSize` = players in the league sets one points
  row per place, from a preset (fedex/even) or custom) /
  cumulative (Stableford total, stroke lowest average) / record (W-L-T,
  win 2 tie 1 by default) / ryder (win 1, halve ½).
- Stableford (`settings.stableford`, rules in `games/stableford.js`, UI in
  `components/StablefordScoring.jsx`, shared with casual rounds): points per result from a preset
  (standard 5/4/3/2/1/0, modified 8/5/2/0/-1/-3) or custom, applied to net or
  gross per `net`. Optional natural bonus (`bonusOn`) adds points for a gross
  birdie/eagle/albatross/ace regardless of strokes; an ace takes the ace bonus
  instead of eagle/albatross. Team best ball takes the higher points.
- Every player needs a Scribble account (supabase/league_pro.sql). The
  commissioner invites by email (`league_members.invite_email`, readable only
  by commissioners via `league_invites()`; column grants hide it from
  members). Signing in with that email links the spot (`claim_my_invites()`
  runs at app start); the invite code / link `/app/?join=CODE` also works
  (`join_league` claims an emailed spot first, then a same-name spot).
  A spot with no `user_id` is "Invited"/"Needs account": it can't be put in
  matches or groups and `can_score()` refuses its card. `team` is the team
  or side name.
- Pairings (round Manage, `PairingsEditor`): the commissioner picks how
  to pair each round: manual, random draw, last round's finish (players
  who missed it follow in standings order), or season standings, with
  leaders teeing off last (default) or first. Group size 4/3/2. Team
  formats keep teammates together; match play can also set the matches
  (neighbours in the order play; odd one out gets no match) and groups
  never split a match. Tee times (`league_events.tee_sheet`): tee times
  from a first time every N minutes, or a shotgun (group i on hole i+1,
  A/B on the first holes when groups outnumber holes); any group's slot
  can be edited. A round with no tee sheet shows no tee times. Players see
  "Your tee time" on the round and in the rounds list, plus the tee sheet
  on Results.
- League Pro (desktop): at 1024px+ `League.jsx` renders `ProConsole`, a
  sidebar console for commissioners: Overview (stats + to-do list),
  Players (sortable table, inline edit, bulk team assign, roster import from
  pasted spreadsheet or CSV), Schedule (build a whole season of rounds at
  once), Score entry (spreadsheet grid, auto-advance, saves as you type),
  Standings (CSV export, print). Rounds open the same LeagueEvent screens.
  Phones keep the mobile League screens.
- Rounds (`league_events`) snapshot the course + tee. Only commissioners
  manage members, rounds, matches and groups, and mark rounds final.
  Players may enter scores only for their own group (`league_events.groups`,
  or their match when no group includes them); commissioners for anyone.
  Enforced by `public.can_score()` (supabase/groups.sql) and mirrored in
  `src/lib/league/groups.js` (keep them in step). Everyone can read all
  scores. Only final rounds count in standings, and
  members can't change scores after final (enforced by RLS).
- Matches (league and casual) stop at the closeout ("4 & 3"). Live match
  play: `matchSummary(status)` gives the scoreboard text (3 UP / ALL SQUARE
  / DORMIE / 4 & 3) and `MatchCard` shows it with hole-by-hole dots, on the
  scoring screen (from the phone's own cards, before sending) and on league
  Results, which re-fetch every 20 s while a round is open. Ryder Cup
  Results add a points board and who's leading on the course. Handicaps: own index or
  league index from earlier final rounds only; allowance %, max handicap,
  strokes off the low player per match.
- Scores go to a localStorage queue first and are sent when online
  (`flushScores`); refused cards are dropped so they can't block the queue.

## Tournaments

- Ryder Cup setup (`RyderSetup`: "Set up" tab on the phone, "Ryder Cup
  setup" in the desktop console; opens first while any round has no
  matches): 1 Teams (Scribble members per side, move/remove, add with
  `AddMembers` team=side), 2 Rounds (how many, and a day, format and course
  per round; picking a course fills later new rounds; a round's course can
  change until scores are in; format saved in `league_events.match_format`),
  3 Matches (pick 2 per side for 2 vs 2 or 1 per side for singles, reorder,
  remove). Foursomes are automatic (`foursomesForMatches`): each 2 vs 2
  match is a foursome; singles pair up 1+2, 3+4, … and are saved as the
  round's groups, so anyone in the foursome can score it.
- Split rounds (Ryder Cup): a round's format can be "Split round" (Rounds
  step): stretches of holes, each with its own format, e.g. 1–6 scramble,
  7–12 four-ball, 13–18 singles (`SplitEditor`; saved as the round's
  `match_format` text `split:1-6:scramble|7-12:fourball|13-18:singles`;
  helpers + tests in league/ryder.js). Matches step: pick a pair vs a pair;
  `splitMatchups` makes one match per stretch (a singles stretch makes two:
  first vs first, second vs second; ⇄ swaps), all sharing a `set` so they
  are one foursome. Each match has `holes: [from, to]`; `scoreMatch` counts
  only those holes (strokes from the full-round allocation) and returns
  `range`. Every stretch is its own point. Score entry uses the match
  covering the current hole (team rows on one-ball stretches), and boards
  label each match "Holes 1–6 · Scramble"; its scorecard shows only those
  holes. Ryder Cup: Manage shows the plan and sends you to Ryder Cup setup
  for matches. Team leagues: Manage's format list has "Split round" with the
  same `SplitEditor` (shared component); Add match / Auto-pair make the
  stretches' matches, ✕ removes a foursome's whole set, and `teeGroups`
  keeps a set as one group. Match cards show only a stretch's holes
  (`MatchCard` `range`).
- Enter scores opens straight on your own foursome's card (at the first
  hole the group hasn't finished); Change group shows the picker
  (commissioners can score any group).
- Score entry layout (no date line, tee time or team board; those are on
  Results): green hole bar (‹ prev / Hole n, par, stroke index,
  tee / next ›), slim hole dots, then one row per player: name (playing
  handicap), score box (stroke dots), this hole's Pts (Stableford) or net,
  and the running total (points, or to par). Tapping a score box opens a
  keypad fixed at the bottom (1–9, Clear, 0, ✕ to hide; Undo changes /
  Save scores) and scrolls that row above it; the
  active score box shows a cursor, a digit enters it and moves to the next
  player (a 1 waits for 0–5 to make 10–15, with a hint above the keypad; the last player keeps the
  cursor). With the keypad closed, Next hole / Review round shows. Undo changes puts the hole
  back as it was when opened; Save scores sends now and, once everyone
  has a score, goes to the next hole (last hole: the round review).
- Leaderboards (round Results and stroke/Stableford `TournamentBoard`)
  use `Leaderboard.jsx`: home club under each name (`homeCourses` in
  social.js), tap a row (or Expand all) for `PlayerCard`: tee/slope/rating, par,
  stroke index, score (circles/boxes, stroke dots), Stableford pts,
  Out/In/Tot/Net. Multi-round boards add Today + R1..Rn; their cards show
  the latest round with scores.
- End of round review (`RoundReview` in LeagueEvent): after the last hole,
  "Review round" (or "Card" anytime) shows the group's full scorecard
  (birdies circled, bogeys boxed, Out/In, totals and to par, match results);
  tap a score to fix that hole; Submit is blocked while scores disagree.
- Ryder Cup Results (`RyderLive` with onOpenMatch, Golf Genius style):
  `TeamPoints` (each side's points per round, total, projected) on top,
  then `MatchBoard`: Pts | side A (names, home clubs) | status | side B |
  Pts per match, the winner's side filled in team color (blue/red) with an
  arrow on the status pointing at the side that's up, halved grey, a
  totals footer for the round. Tapping a match (or Expand all) opens its
  scorecard under the row (`MatchDetail`): "Side (names) vs Side (names)",
  Hole (current hole yellow) / Par, a dark Match row per side (T = tied,
  "2 up" while that side leads) above side A and below side B, each
  player's scores, the team row (Best ball / Combined / Team ball) with
  holes won filled in team color, Out/In/Tot; swipe sideways.
- Multiple scorekeepers: each keeps their own card in `score_entries`
  (`saveScore(…, official, mine)`); the official `event_scores` card is my
  numbers merged over what's there (never wipes others' holes; latest wins).
  Holes I haven't entered show the official score faded ("tap to agree").
  `discrepancies()` flags holes where scorekeepers differ: red hole dot,
  banner, "Mike 3 · You 4" with Use buttons, and "Scores to check" on
  Results.

- Side games (league and tournament rounds, `event.sideGames`,
  supabase/side_games.sql): the commissioner turns on skins (pot buy-in or
  per skin), Stableford points and stroke play in Manage, with stakes, net or
  gross, and everyone or picked players. Results shows each player's money
  and a settle-up ("If it ended now" until every hole is in), live as each
  group scores. Computed by `computeSideGames` with the casual-round engine,
  so the same zero-sum, integer-cents rules apply; a hole counts once
  everyone in has finished it.

- A tournament is a league with `settings.kind = 'tournament'` (same tables,
  invite codes, rounds, scoring screens; no schema change). `rounds` is the
  planned number of rounds. Scoring: stroke or Stableford for players/teams,
  match play for Ryder Cup. Handicaps: own index + allowance/max/off-low (no
  league-handicap mode).
- Standings are live: `tournamentStandings` counts every round as it is
  played, final or not. Stroke/Stableford rows: total (to par or points),
  today, thru, and each round (a round in progress shows score so far with
  holes played). Ryder Cup: points per round, total, and a projection that
  adds matches on the course (leading = 1, all square = ½).
- `TournamentBoard` renders it and refreshes every 20 s while any round is
  open. Matches in team formats need every player assigned a team/side.
- Match formats (`MATCH_FORMATS` in league/event.js), stored per match in
  `league_events.matchups[].format`; the commissioner sets one per round in
  Manage: singles, fourball (best ball), combined score (internal key "shamble", both balls added),
  scramble and foursomes (one team ball). One-ball formats use a team
  handicap (scramble 35% low + 15% high; foursomes half the total) off the
  low team; the team score is saved on every partner's card and score entry
  shows one row per team.
- One scorecard look everywhere (`ScoreTable.jsx`, Golf Genius style):
  Hole / Par header (current hole yellow, problem holes outlined red),
  names pinned left, swipe sideways, Out/In/Tot. Used by league round
  review, leaderboard player cards (+ Stroke index, Stableford pts),
  the casual Card tab (tap a score to go to that hole) and `MatchDetail`
  (same file): a match's card opened under its match card or row (league
  match play, Ryder Cup board, tournament page, casual Money tab), with a
  dark Match row per side (T / "2 up"), players, team row (Best ball /
  Combined / Team ball) with holes won in team color. Matches expose
  `results`, `sideScores` and `running` (league/event.js and
  games/match.js) for it.
- One score entry everywhere (league, tournament and casual `Play`):
  `ScoreKeypad.jsx` (HoleBar, HoleDots, ScoreBox, Keypad) + `useKeypad.js`
  (typing logic).

## Social feed

- Bottom tab bar (`BottomNav`): Golf (rounds/leagues/tournaments), Feed, +
  (new post), Profile. Social screens live in `components/social/` under
  `Social.jsx`, which keeps its own back stack.
- Data (`lib/social.js`, the only code touching these tables and the

  supabase/social.sql. Members can only upload/delete in their own

- Search (`Search.jsx`, from the bar at the top of the Feed): recent
  searches until Enter (typed searches plus profiles/courses opened from
  search; stored on the device per account in `lib/searchHistory.js`, max
  20, "Clear search history"). Enter shows three tabs: For you
  (`searchPosts`: tagged course, caption, or author name; ranked by
  `rankPosts` course > caption > author, newest first), Profiles (name,
  then home course) and Golf courses (OpenGolfAPI search). A course opens
  its location page with an OpenStreetMap embed and an Open in Maps link
  (search results carry lat/lng). Back returns to the same search and tab
  (Social `nav(name, params, keep)` saves params on the current screen).
- Notifications (`notifications` table, supabase/notifications.sql): made
  only by database triggers, never by the app: like (once per liker+post,
  removed on unlike), comment, follow (removed on unfollow), message (one
  row per conversation with an unread count; reading the chat clears it),
  swing review sent/answered. None for your own actions or between blocked
  people. Bell with unread badge on your own profile and a badge on the
  Profile tab (`useUnreadNotifications`: every 30 s, on foreground, and on
  the `scribble:notifications-changed` event). Opening Notifications marks
  all read; grouped Today / This week / Earlier; tap goes to the post,
  chat, profile or Studio. In-app only (no push notifications yet).
- Comments (`PostDetail`): Reply under any comment; replies are one level
  deep (a reply to a reply attaches to the top comment; enforced by a
  trigger), shown indented with the latest 2 and "View N more replies".
  @mentions: typing "@" suggests friends then golfers (`mentionCandidates`),
  `lib/mentions.js` (tested) finds/inserts them, and they render as links to
  the profile. The comment trigger notifies: post owner ('comment'), the
  replied-to author ('reply'), tagged people who can see the post
  ('mention'); one notification per person per comment.
- Adding players to a round (`PlayerSearch` in Setup): the name box
  suggests My players, then friends you follow, then Scribble golfers (2+
  letters; `findGolfers` in social.js); picking one fills the name and the
  handicap index. Indexes come from `handicap_indexes()`
  (supabase/handicap_sharing.sql): only for golfers who share it
  (`privacy_settings.share_handicap`, default on; Settings → Handicap
  index), never between blocked people; GHIN numbers are never shared.
  Typing any name still works for golfers not on Scribble.
- Settings & privacy (supabase/privacy.sql, `getPrivacy`/`savePrivacy` in
  social.js, UI in components/settings/Settings.jsx; the old Profile screen
  is now its "Golf profile" page). Private account
  (`public_profiles.is_private`): posts, media, likes and comments are
  readable only by the owner and accepted followers (`can_see_author()` in
  RLS); following a private account inserts a 'pending' follow (status set by
  a BEFORE trigger, not the app); requests are confirmed/deleted at the top
  of Notifications; going public accepts all pending. Follower counts and
  the Following feed count accepted follows only. `privacy_settings`
  (private to the owner): allow_messages everyone/following/none (checked in
  `start_dm` for new chats and the messages insert policy),
  allow_comments everyone/followers/none (`can_comment()` in the comments
  insert policy; the app hides the box), notify {like, comment, follow,
  message, review} muting (checked by the notification triggers; follow
  requests always notify). `hide_like_counts` hides counts from others.
- Followers / Following: tapping the counts on a profile opens `FollowList`
  (`listFollows` in social.js): both tabs, a search box, Follow /
  Requested / Following buttons, and Remove on your own followers. Accepted
  follows only; blocked people hidden; a private account's lists can't be
  opened by non-followers (counts disabled).
- Saving (private to you): the bookmark saves to `saved_posts` ("All
  saved"); "Add to folder" puts it in your `save_folders` via
  `save_folder_posts` (adding also saves; unsaving also leaves every folder;
  deleting a folder keeps the saves). Own profile -> Saved shows All saved +
  folders (create, rename, delete).
- Sharing: the paper plane opens `ShareSheet`: send the post to golfers by
  DM (search or recent chats, optional note), the phone's share sheet
  (`navigator.share`), or copy the link `/app/?post=ID`. App.jsx reads that
  link once at load and opens the post after sign-in (feed underneath).
- Direct messages (`lib/messages.js`, the only code touching the DM
  tables): one-to-one conversations (`start_dm` reuses an existing one;
  tables allow groups later). Members-only RLS; blocked people can't start
  or send. Inbox from the paper plane on the Feed (unread badge, checked
  every 30 s); Message button on other profiles. Threads poll every 4 s
  (no realtime yet); tapping your own bubble offers Unsend. Read receipts:
  "Sent" / "Seen 2m ago" under your latest message and "· Seen" in the inbox,
  from `conversation_read_times()` (supabase/read_receipts.sql): members
  can't select `last_read_at` directly; the function returns my read time
  and the other person's only when both have read receipts on
  (`privacy_settings.read_receipts`, Settings → Messages; off hides both
  ways, like Instagram). Shared posts
  show as a card that opens the post.
- Viewing: tapping a grid tile (profile or course page) opens `PostList`,
  every post of that grid in one scrolling list starting at the tapped one;
  Back returns to the same scroll spot. Grid tiles are square crops; in the
  feed and lists each post box takes its first item's shape (saved
  width/height, else measured on load), between 9:16 and 1.91:1 and never
  taller than the screen, never cropped. Videos (`FeedVideo`) autoplay muted
  and loop while 60% on screen, pause off screen; a tap toggles sound for
  all videos (`feedSound.js`), double-tap likes.
- Media: photos are shrunk on the phone to 1440px JPEG (`lib/media.js`),
  avatars to 480px; videos upload as-is (50 MB cap) with a frame grabbed as
  the grid cover. Public URLs, newest first everywhere.
- Location pages: posts tagged with a searched course share its
  `oga-<id>` key, so `CoursePage` gathers everyone's photos of that course.
- A username is required before using social; the profile page asks.
- Home course (golf profile and account Settings) uses `HomeCoursePicker`:
  the same course search as rounds/tags, saved with its course key
  (`public_profiles.home_course_key/place`, supabase/home_course.sql) so it
  links to the course page; a typed name (no key) is allowed. Settings
  mirrors its choice onto the public profile (`syncHomeCourse`).
- Blocking (`blocks` table, `block_user()` RPC, supabase/account_safety.sql):
  blocked users are hidden both ways in feed, profiles and comments, and
  follows between them are removed. Unblock in Settings.
- Delete account (Settings): deletes the member's media folder, then
  `delete_my_account()` removes the auth user and cascades; league spots stay
  as name-only players. Required by both stores.
- Reports land in `post_reports`; the terms promise review within 24 hours.
- Website also has /privacy/ and /terms/ (static pages, linked from sign-in,
  Settings and the footer).

## Instructors

Instructors sell monthly subscriptions; Scribble keeps `PLATFORM_FEE_BPS`
(15%) and the instructor the rest (`lib/instructor/fees.js`, integer cents,
split always sums to the price). Full plan and go-live steps:
docs/instructors.md.
- Payments aren't live (`PAYMENTS_LIVE = false`): Subscribe records
  `subscription_interest` ("notify me"). Instructors give free access
  (`source = 'comp'`). Paid rows (`source = 'stripe'`) may only ever be
  written by the Stripe webhook with the service role; RLS lets members
  write comp rows only, and never `verified`, `stripe_*` columns.
- Subscriber-only posts (`posts.audience = 'subscribers'`, only instructors
  may post them) keep media in the private `premium` bucket
  (`<instructorId>/<postId>/…`); `post_media` rows and signed links are only
  given to the author and active subscribers (`has_premium_access`).
  Non-subscribers get `post.locked` and a locked tile.
- Swing reviews: `reviews/<instructorId>/<studentId>/…` in `premium`; the
  tier's `reviews_per_month` is enforced by `reviews_left()`. Answers are
  also sent as a DM. Account deletion removes premium files and review videos.
- Verified badge: set `instructor_profiles.verified` in the Supabase Table
  Editor after checking credentials.

## Course data

- New round starts blank: tapping the empty course search shows Recent
  courses (saved on the phone, last played first); typing shows results,
  with misspellings matched under "Similar names". No games start selected.
  Casual rounds take 1–24 players (`MAX_PLAYERS`, a "How many players"
  dropdown); games are limited by `GAME_LIMITS` (Nassau/match 2–6, skins/
  stroke/Stableford 2–24, Wolf/Vegas 4). Skins can be a pot (buy-in split by
  skins won). 5+ players shows a tip pointing to one-day tournaments.
  The Teams card (4 players) shows below Games only when a chosen game plays
  2 vs 2 (`usesTeams`: Nassau/match/Stableford set to teams, or Vegas).
  Tees: a dropdown ("Blue · 6,850 yds · 72.4/131", plus "+ Add a tee…");
  database courses show rating/slope/yards with Edit tee, hand-entered
  courses keep the edit boxes open. Imported tees carry `yards`.
  No separate Course name box: a course not in the database is added from
  the search box ("+ Use “…” as a new course", CourseSearch `onCustom`).
  League/tournament rounds (Add round on the phone, Schedule builder on
  desktop) use the same picker via `CoursePicker` (recents on tap, close
  matches, new course, tees dropdown).

Course search uses OpenGolfAPI (https://api.opengolfapi.org/api/v1),
called straight from the phone (CORS is open).
- No API key in the app: search and detail work without one (per-device daily
  limit), and anything shipped in a public PWA is readable by anyone. Keep the
  user's key out of the repo; it's only useful later from a server.
- Data is ODbL 1.0. Show the attribution `© OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL
  1.0) via OpenGolfAPI` wherever results appear. A shared course database
  built from it (e.g. in Supabase) must also be ODbL.
- Data is community-maintained: `courseImport.js` drops stray holes past 18,
  plays 9-hole courses twice (stroke index n -> 2n-1 out, 2n in), doubles
  9-hole ratings, and fills missing pars/handicaps/ratings with defaults plus a
  warning shown in setup. Picked courses save to IndexedDB with id `oga-<id>`,
  so a course you've fixed keeps your fixes and works offline.

## Rules

- Members only: everyone who plays (casual rounds, leagues, tournaments)
  needs a Scribble account; the goal is that everyone downloads the app.
  Casual rounds: row 1 is you (locked), every other player must be picked
  from the list (recent players saved on the phone in `lib/recentGolfers.js`,
  friends, Scribble members); typed names that aren't picked block tee off,
  and "Invite someone to Scribble" shares the app link. Leagues/tournaments:
  "+ Add Scribble members" (`AddMembers`, `addAccounts` in leagueApi) adds
  people linked to their accounts; email invites remain for people not on
  Scribble yet.

- The game/money engine (Nassau, presses, skins, Wolf, Vegas, handicap strokes)
  is pure functions with tests. Money math must never be wrong.
- The app tracks and settles bets; it never holds or moves money. Venmo-style
  links only. (Instructor subscriptions are separate: Stripe will hold and
  pay out that money; the app never touches card details. See
  docs/instructors.md.)
- GHIN handicaps need licensing; until then, self-reported indexes. Courses
  come from OpenGolfAPI search or are entered by hand.
- Setup in under 60 seconds. Big touch targets. Plain-English game rules.
