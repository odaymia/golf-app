# Scribble Golf Club

Scribble Golf Club (repo `golf-app`) is a golf app for leagues, daily tournaments, and foursomes playing betting games
on the course. Goal: everything Golf Genius does, but far easier to use. Ships
with a website (marketing page + public league/event pages).

Separate product from the shop apps (time clock, Shop Desk). Own repo, own
deploy, own Supabase project.

## Plan

1. Foursome MVP: pick course, add players, pick games, hole-by-hole scoring,
   live settle-up ("Mike owes Dave $14"). **Built (Phase 1).**
2. Leagues: season schedule, pairings, standings, points, league handicaps.
   **Built:** league builder, invite codes, rounds, matches, standings.
3. Tournaments: flights, shotgun/tee times, big leaderboard, side pots, payouts.
   **Built:** tournaments (one-day or multi-round; stroke, Stableford, teams,
   Ryder Cup) with live leaderboards. Not yet: flights, tee times, payouts.
4. Business: club/organizer accounts, branding, paid tiers, App Store builds.
   **Started:** Capacitor iPhone/Android projects, store prep in docs/app-store.md.

## Stack

Two pages from one Vite build: the website at `/` (`index.html`, static HTML
+ `src/site/`) and the app at `/app/` (`app/index.html`). The app is Vite 5 +
React 18 (plain JS, no router) as an offline-first PWA
(`vite-plugin-pwa`, service worker precaches the app). Rounds and courses live
on the device in IndexedDB. Supabase project `qdnrmeipmggpxynggsnl` holds
accounts, profiles, saved players, leagues and shared casual rounds
(realtime leaderboards come later). Capacitor later for App Store / Play Store.

  GoDaddy: `@` A records to GitHub Pages (185.199.108-111.153), `www` CNAME to
  `odaymia.github.io`. Custom domain + Enforce HTTPS are set in repo

  Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen (on the website too).

  `src/polyfills.js` (loaded first in main.jsx, tested) adds `.at()` and
  `structuredClone` when missing. CSS: don't put `:has()` in a selector list
  with other selectors (an unsupported selector drops the whole rule); give
  container-query units (`cqw`) a fallback.

  often after the keyboard, so `position: fixed; bottom: 0` bars float

  still while the page bounces. `html, body { overscroll-behavior-y: none }`

- Crashes: `ErrorBoundary` (main.jsx) shows Reload / Go to home screen

- Updates: the service worker auto-updates, and `main.jsx` checks for a new

  resume rather than relaunch) and every 30 min. An update reloads the page;
  `App.jsx` reopens the round you were on (`lib/place.js`, localStorage).

### Engine conventions

- All money is integer cents. Every game returns `balances` `{playerId: cents}`
  that must sum to exactly zero; `computeRound` throws if one doesn't.
- Game configs live on the round (`round.games[]`), including in-round state
  (Nassau `presses`, Wolf `decisions`), so a round is one JSON document.
- Games score "as if the round ended now": results update hole by hole.
  Nassau and match status count holes in order and stop at the first gap.
- Plus handicaps are stored as negative indexes (+2.1 -> -2.1).
- Rule choices (document any change here and in the module header):
  - Nassau auto-press: a new press starts the hole after the latest bet in that
    nine goes N down, only if a hole is left in the nine. The 18-hole bet is not
    auto-pressed unless `pressOverall`. Presses pay the segment stake.
  - Team bets (2v2): each winner collects the stake, each loser pays it.
  - Skins per-skin: each other player pays the winner. Validation = winner must
    tie or win the next hole; skins on 18 need none; ties after 18 go unwon.
  - Wolf: lone/blind wolf wins or loses stake x multiplier with each of the 3.
  - Vegas: a score of 10+ goes first; birdie flips the other team unless both
    teams birdie; net scores below 1 count as 1.
  - Paying by place (casual stroke play and individual Stableford, mode
    'places'; `payByPlace` in games/places.js, tested): `stake` is each
    player's entry, the pot pays 1st/2nd/3rd… by percent (`places`), ranked
    on holes everyone has finished (stroke: lowest total; Stableford: most
    points); ties split via `computePayoutsCents` (league/payouts.js);
    balances = winnings − entry; nothing owed before a full hole. Setup:
    "By place" next to the other payout buttons (entry defaults to $20).
    Old rounds with a stand-alone `places` game still score (`scorePlaces`).
  - Split match (casual, 2 vs 2 teams only): `game.segments`
    `[{ from, to, format }]` (best ball 'fourball', combined 'shamble',
    'singles'); each stretch is its own bet worth `stake`, a singles stretch
    is two bets (first vs first, second vs second). Paid as of now like every
    match. Setup: "Split the round" switch under Match play (2 vs 2 teams);
    no scramble/alternate shot in casual rounds (scores are per player).

## Shared casual rounds

- A casual round goes to every player's phone (supabase/casual_rounds.sql,
  run in the SQL Editor). Rounds where every player has an account
  (members only, so all new ones) are `round.shared` with `ownerId`;
  older rounds without accounts stay on the one phone as before.
- Foursomes: 5+ players get a "Foursomes" card in Setup (even split in
  player order, `defaultGroups`: 5 -> 3+2, 8 -> 4+4; move anyone with
  the group dropdown; 4 at most per group). Saved as `round.groups`.
  **Each player keeps score only for their own foursome** (`myGroupIds`;
  enforced by `can_score_casual()` in `put_casual_entries`); the other
  groups show read-only under yours on the Score tab. **Side games include
  everyone** in the round (skins, stroke, Stableford across groups), and
  Money/Settle show the whole field. Presses show for matches your
  foursome is in. Next hole / hole dots go by your foursome.
- Sync model (lib/casualSync.js, tested): the setup (course, tee, players,
  games, groups) is fixed at tee-off and sent once (`create_casual_round`).
  Everything that changes is an entry, one per key: `s:<player>:<hole>`
  score (null = cleared, so it can't come back), `w:<game>:<hole>` Wolf
  pick, `p:<game>:<matchup>:<segment>:<hole>` press, `status` (Final),
  `recap`, `pr:<name>`. Each carries `at` (phone clock, ms); newest wins per
  key on the phone (`mergeServer`) and on the server (the upsert only
  replaces an older `at`), so one phone's edits never wipe another's and a
  change that sat offline can't undo a newer one. Each phone keeps
  `known` per round in localStorage (`golf-app:casual-known:<id>`); App
  diffs the round against it on every change (`trackLocal`) and queues the
  differences (`golf-app:casual-queue`), sent 0.4 s later or when signal
  returns. Only a refusal by our SQL (P0001: not a player, round deleted)
  drops queued changes; no signal, an expired sign-in or a missing SQL
  function keeps them. Current hole and "rated" stay per phone.
- Other phones: Play/Settle pull the round every 20 s, on foreground and
  when back online (`pullRound`); Home pulls every round I'm in
  (`pullMyRounds`: new ones saved, changed ones merged, deleted/hidden ones
  removed). A shared round opened for the first time starts at the first
  hole your foursome hasn't finished. "Saved on this phone. N changes will
  send when there's signal." shows on the Score tab while any wait.
- Anyone in the round can mark it Final (and fix scores after). Delete:
  the creator's ✕ deletes it for everyone; anyone else's ✕ removes it from
  their own list (`hide_casual_round`). Both need signal once the round
  has reached the server.

## Round recap and course ratings

- After a round (casual: Settle once every hole is in; league/tournament:
  round review after Submit scores) the app shows an AI recap that spreads
  hype and ribbing across the group (good and bad holes, nines, money, not
  just first and last), one per round (no rewrites; Try again only after an
  error), then "Rate <course>": 1-5 stars for
  Conditions, Layout, Price (5 = great value), Difficulty (5 = tough),
  Pace of play (5 = no waiting; column added later, older ratings have none).
- Recap: `recapFacts` (lib/roundRecap.js) sends only names, scores, results,
  best/worst holes and money as text; the `round-recap` Edge Function checks
  sign-in, calls `claim_recap()` (20/day per person), and asks Claude
  (`claude-opus-5-5`, effort low, server-side refusal fallback). The key
  never ships in the app. One group recap per round, plus "Your own recap":
  each player taps their name for a recap of just their round (`focus` =
  their name; written only when tapped, once). Casual: saved on the round
  (`round.recap`, `round.playerRecaps[name]`). League/tournament: shared in
  `round_recaps` (one per foursome, group_key = sorted player keys; one per
  player, `player:<key>`) via `start_round_recap()` / `finish_round_recap()`
  so the group pays once and sees the same text (a groupmate's in-progress
  recap is waited for), cached in localStorage. Deploy: Supabase
  dashboard -> Edge Functions -> new function `round-recap` (paste the
  file), Secrets -> ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.
- Ratings (`course_ratings`, one per golfer per course, re-rating replaces)
  only for shared course ids (`oga-<id>`, built-in `sc-…`); league round
  snapshots now keep `course.key`; older rounds without one find it by
  exact name (`resolveCourseKey`: built-in courses, then course search; only
  a single exact match counts); with no exact match the card asks "Which
  course did you play?" from close matches (`courseCandidates`).
  Members read only their own rows; averages come from
  `course_rating_summary(keys)`. Overall stars = conditions/layout/price/pace
  (difficulty shown, not averaged in). Shown as "★ 4.3 (12)" in course
  search (round setup and social Search) and as a block on the course page
  above its recent photos.

## Accounts

- Sign-in is a 6-digit email code (`signInWithOtp` + `verifyOtp`), not a
  magic link (password sign-in also exists, mainly for the store reviewers'
  demo account): a link would open Safari instead of the home-screen app. The
  Supabase email templates (Magic Link and Confirm signup) must include
  `{{ .Token }}`. The built-in mailer allows only a few emails per hour; add
  custom SMTP before a league signs up.
- The session is stored on the phone, so the app opens signed in with no
  signal. First sign-in requires a name (first-time Profile screen).
- `profiles` (one per user) and `saved_players` (private to the owner) are
  protected by row level security in `supabase/schema.sql`. Schema changes go
  in that file; the user runs it in the SQL Editor.
- Never ship the service_role/secret key. GHIN: handicap index and GHIN number
  are self-reported until the USGA approves an integration; that integration
  must run server-side (e.g. a Supabase Edge Function), never in the app.

## Leagues

- `leagues.settings` (jsonb) holds the format; `normalizeSettings` fills
  defaults and drops invalid combos. Units: individual / teams (2 or 4) /
  ryder (two named sides). Scoring: stroke / stableford / match, net or
  gross. Season: points (by finish, ties split, optional best-N weeks;
  FedEx Cup style: each round is `tier` regular or major, majors pay
  `majorMultiplier`x; `fieldSize` = players in the league sets one points
  row per place, from a preset (fedex/even) or custom) /
  cumulative (Stableford total, stroke lowest average) / record (W-L-T,
  win 2 tie 1 by default) / ryder (win 1, halve ½; or the round's / stretch's
  points per match).
- Stableford (`settings.stableford`, rules in `games/stableford.js`, UI in
  `components/StablefordScoring.jsx`, shared with casual rounds): points per result from a preset
  (standard 5/4/3/2/1/0, modified 8/5/2/0/-1/-3) or custom, applied to net or
  gross per `net`. Optional natural bonus (`bonusOn`) adds points for a gross
  birdie/eagle/albatross/ace regardless of strokes; an ace takes the ace bonus
  instead of eagle/albatross. Team best ball takes the higher points.
- Not playing / watching (supabase/watchers.sql): `league_members.plays`
  (false = in the league but not a player) and role 'viewer'. Creating a
  league/tournament has "I'm playing in this" (off = organizer runs it
  without a card; also a switch under Players → Not playing). One Join
  button and one invite link for everyone (supabase/join_code.sql):
  `join_league` gives you the spot reserved for you (email, then name, or
  your account already added); with no spot you join as a viewer who reads
  everything but can't score (`can_score()` refuses viewers and
  non-players) and sees no Enter scores tab. The commissioner moves a
  viewer into the field with "Add as player" (`addToField`) under Players
  → Not playing; viewers can "Stop watching" (`stop_watching()`). Old
  `?watch=CODE` links still work (same as join). `loadLeague` gives `data.members` =
  players only (standings, groups, cards) and `data.people` = everyone; `me`
  comes from people; anyone in a match or with a score card counts as a
  player whatever their switch says (and the switch won't turn off then:
  it's disabled with the rounds named under it; empty cards don't count);
  computeEvent skips a match naming someone not in the field. A non-playing commissioner's Enter scores opens the
  any-group picker. Home leagues list shows Watching / Organizer badges;
  home Tee off/RSVP cards skip non-playing spots.
- Every player needs a Scribble account (supabase/league_pro.sql). The
  commissioner invites by email (`league_members.invite_email`, readable only
  by commissioners via `league_invites()`; column grants hide it from
  members). Signing in with that email links the spot (`claim_my_invites()`
  runs at app start); the invite code / link `/app/?join=CODE` also works
  (`join_league` claims an emailed spot first, then a same-name spot).
  A spot with no `user_id` is "Invited"/"Needs account": it can't be put in
  matches or groups and `can_score()` refuses its card. `team` is the team
  or side name.
- Pairings (round Manage, `PairingsEditor`): the commissioner picks how
  to pair each round: manual, random draw, last round's finish (players
  who missed it follow in standings order), or season standings, with
  leaders teeing off last (default) or first. Group size 4/3/2. Team
  formats keep teammates together; match play can also set the matches
  (neighbours in the order play; odd one out gets no match) and groups
  never split a match. Tee times (`league_events.tee_sheet`): tee times
  from a first time every N minutes, or a shotgun (group i on hole i+1,
  A/B on the first holes when groups outnumber holes); any group's slot
  can be edited. A round with no tee sheet shows no tee times. Players see
  "Your tee time" on the round and in the rounds list, plus the tee sheet
  on Results.
- League Pro (desktop): at 1024px+ `League.jsx` renders `ProConsole`, a
  sidebar console for commissioners: Overview (stats + to-do list),
  Players (sortable table, inline edit, bulk team assign, roster import from
  pasted spreadsheet or CSV), Schedule (build a whole season of rounds at
  once), Score entry (spreadsheet grid, auto-advance, saves as you type),
  Standings (CSV export, print). Rounds open the same LeagueEvent screens.
  Phones keep the mobile League screens.
- Rounds (`league_events`) snapshot the course + tee. Only commissioners
  manage members, rounds, matches and groups, and mark rounds final.
  Players may enter scores only for their own group (`league_events.groups`,
  or their match when no group includes them); commissioners for anyone.
  Enforced by `public.can_score()` (supabase/groups.sql) and mirrored in
  `src/lib/league/groups.js` (keep them in step). Everyone can read all
  scores. Only final rounds count in standings, and
  members can't change scores after final (enforced by RLS).
- Matches (league and casual) stop at the closeout ("4 & 3"). Live match
  play: `matchSummary(status)` gives the scoreboard text (3 UP / ALL SQUARE
  / DORMIE / 4 & 3) and `MatchCard` shows it with hole-by-hole dots, on the
  scoring screen (from the phone's own cards, before sending) and on league
  Results, which re-fetch every 20 s while a round is open. Ryder Cup
  Results add a points board and who's leading on the course. Handicaps: own index or
  league index from earlier final rounds only; allowance %, max handicap,
  strokes off the low player per match.
- Scores go to a localStorage queue first and are sent when online
  (`flushScores`); refused cards are dropped so they can't block the queue.

## Instructors

  (`<instructorId>/<postId>/…`); `post_media` rows and signed links are only
  given to the author and active subscribers (`has_premium_access`).
  Non-subscribers get `post.locked` and a locked tile.
- Swing reviews: `reviews/<instructorId>/<studentId>/…` in `premium`; the
  tier's `reviews_per_month` is enforced by `reviews_left()`. Answers are
  also sent as a DM. Account deletion removes premium files and review videos.
- Verified badge: set `instructor_profiles.verified` in the Supabase Table

## Course data

- New round starts blank: tapping the empty course search shows Recent
  courses (saved on the phone, last played first); typing shows results,
  with misspellings matched under "Similar names". No games start selected.
  Casual rounds take 1–8 players (`MAX_PLAYERS`, a "How many players"
  dropdown); games are limited by `GAME_LIMITS` (Nassau/match 2–6, skins/
  stroke/Stableford 2+ (any field size, so league side games work), Wolf/Vegas 4). Skins can be a pot (buy-in split by
  skins won). 5+ players get the Foursomes card (each group scores on its
  own phone; see Shared casual rounds) and a tip pointing to one-day
  tournaments for leaderboards.
  The Teams card (4 players) shows below Games only when a chosen game plays
  2 vs 2 (`usesTeams`: Nassau/match/Stableford set to teams, or Vegas).
  Tees: a dropdown ("Blue · 6,850 yds · 72.4/131", plus "+ Add a tee…");
  database courses show rating/slope/yards with Edit tee, hand-entered
  courses keep the edit boxes open. Imported tees carry `yards`.
  No separate Course name box: a course not in the database is added from
  the search box ("+ Use “…” as a new course", CourseSearch `onCustom`).
  League/tournament rounds (Add round on the phone, Schedule builder on
  desktop) use the same picker via `CoursePicker` (recents on tap, close
  matches, new course, tees dropdown).

Course search uses only OpenGolfAPI (https://api.opengolfapi.org/api/v1),
called straight from the phone (CORS is open); when it's down, only courses
saved on the phone show. Not used (Nov 2026, the owner's call): our own copy
of the open data, `public.courses` + `search_courses()` in Supabase
(supabase/courses.sql, still loaded): the open data has no tee ratings or
slopes. Turning it back on would mean searching it in courseSearch.js.
- Shared course backup (supabase/course_backup.sql, `lib/courseBackup.js`,
  tested): every course picked from OpenGolfAPI is saved (the API's full
  answer, via `save_course_backup()`, signed-in only, openly readable per
  ODbL). When OpenGolfAPI fails, `searchCourses` searches the backup and
  `fetchCourse` loads from it (same ids, so `oga-<id>` keys, ratings and
  photos still line up); with nothing in the backup the "isn't responding"
  message shows as before. Grows with the courses members play (no bulk
  download: the open data dump has no ratings/slopes). GHIN (USGA) has
  ratings/slopes for every rated course; that's the long-term source once
  licensed (server-side only).
- When OpenGolfAPI is down (it returns 500/522 after ~20 s), requests give
  up after 8 s and say the course database isn't responding (not a status
  code). CourseSearch always lists matching courses saved on the phone
  first ("Saved on this phone", `matchSaved`, typo-tolerant), so recent
  courses and "+ Use … as a new course" keep working offline or in an outage.
- No API key in the app: search and detail work without one (per-device daily
  limit), and anything shipped in a public PWA is readable by anyone. Keep the
  user's key out of the repo; it's only useful later from a server.
- Data is ODbL 1.0. Show the attribution (`ATTRIBUTION` in courseImport.js:
  `© OpenStreetMap contributors · contains data from OpenGolfAPI
  (opengolfapi.org) · ODbL 1.0`) wherever results appear. Our Supabase copy
  is a derived database, so it must stay ODbL and openly readable.
- Built-in courses (`lib/builtinCourses.js`, tested): full cards with real
  tees/ratings/slopes, always in search under "Courses in Scribble" (even
  when OpenGolfAPI is down); picking one goes through `onPick` like an API
  course. Now: Steele Canyon (Jamul, CA) Vineyard/Canyon, Canyon/Ranch,
  Ranch/Vineyard; Singing Hills Golf Resort at Sycuan (El Cajon, CA) Willow
  Glen and Oak Glen (GolfPass ratings, Nov 2026). Add more there.
- Data gaps: a card numbered past 9
  is 18 holes; missing pars become 4 and, when most stroke indexes are known
  without repeats, missing holes get the unused ones; warnings name the holes.
- Data is community-maintained: `courseImport.js` drops stray holes past 18,
  plays 9-hole courses twice (stroke index n -> 2n-1 out, 2n in), doubles
  9-hole ratings, and fills missing pars/handicaps/ratings with defaults plus a
  warning shown in setup. Picked courses save to IndexedDB with id `oga-<id>`,
  so a course you've fixed keeps your fixes and works offline.

## Rules

- Members only: everyone who plays (casual rounds, leagues, tournaments)
  needs a Scribble account; the goal is that everyone downloads the app.
  Casual rounds: row 1 is you (locked), every other player must be picked
  from the list (recent players saved on the phone in `lib/recentGolfers.js`,
  friends, Scribble members); typed names that aren't picked block tee off,
  and "Invite someone to Scribble" shares the app link. Leagues/tournaments:
  "+ Add Scribble members" (`AddMembers`, `addAccounts` in leagueApi) adds
  people linked to their accounts; email invites remain for people not on
  Scribble yet.

- The game/money engine (Nassau, presses, skins, Wolf, Vegas, handicap strokes)
  is pure functions with tests. Money math must never be wrong.
- The app tracks and settles bets; it never holds or moves money. Venmo-style
  links only. (Instructor subscriptions are separate: Stripe will hold and
  pay out that money; the app never touches card details. See
  docs/instructors.md.)
- GHIN handicaps need licensing; until then, self-reported indexes. Courses
  come from OpenGolfAPI search or are entered by hand.
- Setup in under 60 seconds. Big touch targets. Plain-English game rules.
