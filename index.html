The Games

Every game your group already plays

Pick your games and stakes in under a minute. Handicap strokes, presses and carryovers are handled for you, and the money updates after every hole.

No. 1

Nassau

Front, back and overall, with manual presses or automatic presses when you go two down.

No. 2

Skins

Gross or net, with carryovers and optional validation. Every skin tracked to the hole.

No. 3

Wolf

The rotation is set for you. Pick a partner, go lone wolf for double, or blind wolf for triple.

No. 4

Vegas

Team numbers built automatically, double digits handled, and a birdie flips the other side.

No. 5

Stableford

Standard, Modified or your own points, solo or two-on-two, with bonuses for natural birdies.

No. 6

Match & Stroke Play

One-on-one, best ball or everyone against everyone, net or gross.

Settle Up

One tap. The fewest payments possible.

Scribble nets every game against every player and works out the fewest payments to square everyone up. No napkin math in the grill room, no arguments about the presses.

  • Exact to the penny, every game, every time
  • Live “if we stopped now” totals during the round
  • Send the results to the group text in one tap
  • Scribble never holds or moves money. Pay each other however you like.

Leagues

Build the league you actually want to run

Set it up once, share an invite code, and Scribble keeps the schedule, the scores and the standings.

Any Format

Single players, two- or four-player teams, or a Ryder Cup between two sides. Stroke play, Stableford or match play.

Season Points

FedEx Cup-style points by finish for every round, with majors worth more. Count every week or only your best.

Match Play Records

Head-to-head leagues with wins, losses and halves, and an auto-pair schedule so everyone meets everyone.

Fair Handicaps

Each player’s own index, or a league handicap from their recent league rounds. Allowance and caps included.

Invite in Seconds

Members join with a six-letter code. Add players without the app too: their spot is waiting when they join.

Live Standings

Results update as scores come in. The commissioner marks a round final and the standings lock in.

Made for the Course

Fast, simple, and it works with no signal

Big Buttons

Tap a score, tap next. Built to be used with a glove on and the cart rolling.

Works Offline

Scores save on your phone and send when you’re back in range. Dead zones don’t lose a hole.

Course Search

How It Works

On the first tee in under a minute

  1. 1

    Pick the Course

    Search for it, choose your tees, and add your group with their handicaps.

  2. 2

    Choose Your Games

    Turn on the games you play and set the stakes. Smart defaults get you started.

  3. 3

    Score and Settle

    One phone keeps score for everyone. After eighteen, see who pays who.

Put it on your home screen

On iPhone, open scribblegolfclub.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app.

On Android, open scribblegolfclub.com in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). Keep Chrome up to date in the Play Store.
