Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Scribble Golf Club (“Scribble”, “we”) makes an app for scoring golf rounds, running leagues and tournaments, and sharing golf photos and videos. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Golf profile: your name, handicap index, GHIN number (optional), home course and the players you save. Your handicap index and GHIN number are private to you.
  • Public profile: your username, display name, bio, profile photo and home course. Other Scribble members can see these.
  • Posts: photos, videos, captions, course tags, likes, comments and who you follow. Other Scribble members can see your posts.
  • Leagues and tournaments: the events you join and the scores you or your group enter. Members of the same event can see them.
  • Casual rounds: scores and games for casual rounds are stored on your device, not on our servers.
  • Reports and blocks: posts you report and accounts you block.

We do not collect your precise location. A course tag is the course you choose, not your GPS position. We do not collect contacts, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, scoring, handicap strokes, settle-ups, leagues, tournaments and the feed.
  • To keep Scribble safe: reviewing reported content and enforcing our Terms of use.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo/video storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and web app.
  • OpenGolfAPI provides course information when you search for a course. Your search words are sent to it; your account details are not.

Money and betting

Scribble keeps score of friendly games between players and shows who owes whom. Scribble never collects, holds or moves money, and we don’t collect any payment details for those games.

Your choices

  • Edit your profiles at any time in the app.
  • Delete any post or comment you made.
  • Block other members so you won’t see each other.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profiles, posts, photos, videos, likes, comments, follows and blocks. League and tournament results you were part of stay, under your name, so other members’ standings still add up.

Children

Scribble is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for big changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@scribblegolfclub.com
